Scene 3 · The reveal

Here's what just ran underneath.

There's no magic here, just a structure that gets built once, in a deliberate order. Context comes first and agents come second, because the repositories have to exist before an agent can be pointed at them.

1

Your context, captured

Pricing logic, past bids, project sheets, your voice: tiered, governed, one source of truth. This is the asset. It stays when people leave.

2

Pointed agents, in teams

Not one chatbot. A team per function, each agent reading only its slice: takeoff, quote, proposal, check. Narrow beats general.

3

Your rules, around everything

Approval gates where money, risk, or your name goes out the door. Permissions mirror your org. Every action logged. You stay in control.

Context first, agents second — the order is the method.
