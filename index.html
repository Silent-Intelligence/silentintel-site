Only one person knows why the process works.

These look like separate problems. In my experience they are one problem wearing three costumes — a workflow that is not visible enough to improve, or to hand to anyone else.

I spent twenty-five years in and around technology and engineering organizations, more than twelve of them leading consulting projects for Fortune 500 companies. I was trained in philosophy, linguistics, and literary criticism, which is why I look at a workflow as a human system before I look at it as a technical one. I co-authored The Silent Intelligence in 2013, one of the first books on the Internet of Things.

Good technology recedes into the work. The results should be visible; the machinery should not.

That is the whole idea, and it takes about a year of Tuesdays to explain properly.
